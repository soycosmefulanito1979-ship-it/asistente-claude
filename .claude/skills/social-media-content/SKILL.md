# Social Media Content & Scheduling

**Purpose:** Generate book promotion content calendars, captions, posting strategies, and audience engagement tactics for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.

## When to Use This Skill

- "Create a 4-week content calendar for my books"
- "Write captions for this book excerpt across languages"
- "What's the best posting schedule for indie authors?"
- "Design a content pillar strategy for book promotion"
- "Generate 10 hook ideas for TikTok book promos"

## How It Works

### 1. Content Calendar Generation

**Input:** Book title, platform, target audience, duration (e.g., 4 weeks)

**Output:** Formatted calendar with:
- Posting dates and times (optimized per platform)
- Content type (carousel, video, quote, behind-the-scenes, excerpt, story)
- Caption skeleton (headline + 2-3 bullet points)
- Visual description (what to film/design)
- Call-to-action (CTA) suggestion

**Example:**
```
Date: Tuesday, June 10 @ 7 PM
Platform: Instagram
Type: Carousel (3 slides)
Visual: Book cover + 2 quote pages
Caption: "Have you ever loved someone you couldn't be with? 💔 
This is the heart of 'An Unfinished Love Story' — a novel that 
asks: what if the ending isn't the end? Tap to discover more."
CTA: "Link in bio to read the first chapter free"
```

### 2. Caption Writing (Multilingual)

**Input:** Content type, book excerpt or theme, language (Spanish/Italian/English)

**Output:** 
- Hook (first 2 lines that stop scrollers)
- Body (emotional connection + intrigue)
- CTA (action step)
- Hashtag suggestions (15-20 per language)

**Applies:** Maintains your voice — flowing prose, close to audience, attention-grabbing, no short choppy sentences.

### 3. Posting Strategy

**Input:** Your 3 platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)

**Output:**
- Optimal posting frequency per platform
- Best times to post (by timezone/audience)
- Content mix % (promotional vs. behind-the-scenes vs. educational)
- Platform-specific strengths and how to leverage them

### 4. Content Pillars

**Purpose:** Ensure consistency and variety without overthinking what to post.

**Your 4 Content Pillars for Book Promotion:**
1. **Story & Emotion** — Excerpts, themes, character moments (40%)
2. **Behind-the-Book** — Writing process, inspiration, author life (25%)
3. **Reader Community** — Testimonials, reader reactions, fan content (20%)
4. **Calls to Action** — Buy links, free chapters, email signup (15%)

### 5. Series Ideas (Easy Repeatable Content)

**#AuthorLife Mondays:** 10-min behind-the-scenes writing clips
**#ExcerptWednesday:** Stunning quote from your book + story
**#FridayThoughtful:** Deeper theme exploration from a book
**Multilingual Sundays:** Repost best content in all 3 languages

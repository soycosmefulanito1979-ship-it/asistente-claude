# Indie Writer Growth Analysis

**Purpose:** Research successful independent authors, extract their growth tactics, and synthesize actionable strategies for your book promotion.

## When to Use This Skill

- "Analyze 5 indie authors in the romance/literary fiction niche"
- "What are indie authors doing on TikTok that works?"
- "Compare marketing strategies across BookTok vs. Instagram vs. TikTok"
- "Find indie authors selling books in multiple languages"
- "What's the winning formula for indie book launches?"

## How It Works

### 1. Competitor Profile Research

**Input:** Author name or genre/niche (e.g., "indie romance authors", "multilingual fiction writers")

**Output:** Profile document for each author with:
- **Platform presence:** Instagram followers, TikTok followers, Facebook engagement rate
- **Content strategy:** What they post (frequency, format, style)
- **Engagement tactics:** How they talk to readers (comments, live streams, Q&As)
- **Monetization:** Books available, pricing, DTC vs. Amazon KDP vs. other channels
- **Unique positioning:** What makes them stand out
- **Growth phase:** Emerging, scaling, or established

**Example Profile:**
```
Author: [Name]
Niche: Contemporary romance
Book count: 6 published
Primary platform: TikTok (150K followers, #BookTok)
Content mix: 60% short story clips, 30% author vlog, 10% reader testimonials
Posting frequency: 5-7 TikToks/week
Engagement: Responds to 80% of comments, hosts weekly live Q&As
Revenue model: Self-published on Amazon KDP, $200K+ annual estimated
Unique angle: Emphasizes mental health themes, very responsive community
```

### 2. Tactic Extraction

**Purpose:** Pull out the specific, reproducible tactics from successful authors.

**Common Tactics to Document:**
- **Content formats that win:** Trending sounds + book excerpts? Short clips? Story-driven POVs?
- **Engagement loops:** How do they get readers to take action (follow, buy, share)?
- **Community building:** Do they have Discord? Email list? Patreon?
- **Launch strategies:** How do they launch new books? Pre-order campaigns? Street team tactics?
- **Cross-platform synergy:** Do they repurpose content? Link strategies?
- **Monetization funnels:** Book → email list → Patreon → merchandise?

### 3. Competitive Matrix

**Output:** Side-by-side comparison of 3-5 indie authors showing:
- Platform strength (where they dominate)
- Content strategy (what type of content works for them)
- Audience size (followers, engagement rate %)
- Estimated revenue (based on visible sales signals)
- Applicable to your goals? (Yes/No + why)

**Example:**
| Author | Platform | Content | Followers | Revenue | Applicable? |
|--------|----------|---------|-----------|---------|------------|
| Author A | TikTok | Story clips | 200K | $500K | Yes - multilingual potential |
| Author B | Instagram | Behind-book | 80K | $150K | Maybe - less social-first |
| Author C | YouTube | Author vlogs | 50K | $200K | Yes - personality-driven |

### 4. Strategy Synthesis

**Purpose:** Take what you learned and create actionable recommendations for YOUR books.

**Synthesis Output:**
- **Quick wins:** 1-2 tactics you can implement immediately
- **Medium-term plays:** Strategies requiring 4-8 weeks setup
- **Long-term positioning:** How to differentiate your 6 books from competitors
- **Risk assessment:** Which tactics have high ROI vs. time-intensive but lower payoff

**Example Synthesis:**
```
Quick wins:
✓ Use BookTok trending sounds with your book excerpts (high engagement, low effort)
✓ Post 3-4x weekly on TikTok (Author A does 5-7, but 3-4 sustainable for solo)

Medium-term:
→ Build email list via free first chapter + bonus content
→ Create "multilingual reading" angle (unique to your 3-language books)

Long-term:
→ Position as "international indie author" across Spanish/Italian/English communities
→ Develop reader community Discord or Telegram channel
```

## How to Invoke This Skill

**Say:** "Research indie authors selling [genre/niche] and extract growth tactics"

**Or:** "Analyze what's working on BookTok for indie fiction authors"

**Or:** "Compare 3 indie authors who write in multiple languages"

## What You'll Get

- 3-5 detailed competitor profiles (strategies documented)
- Extractable, reproducible tactics with implementation steps
- Comparative analysis showing what's working where
- Customized recommendations based on your 6 books + 3-language positioning
- Monthly research updates as the indie author landscape shifts

## Research Methodology

- **Primary sources:** TikTok, Instagram, author websites, Goodreads, BookTok community signals
- **Data points:** Follower counts, post frequency, engagement rate %, estimated sales (where visible)
- **Limitations:** Some revenue data estimated; strategies evolving rapidly in indie space
- **Update frequency:** Refresh quarterly or when major platform algorithm changes

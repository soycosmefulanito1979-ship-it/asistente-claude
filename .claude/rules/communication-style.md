# Communication Style

**Audience:** Carlos Rubino

## Writing Preferences

- **Sentence structure:** Avoid short, fragmented sentences. Prefer flowing paragraphs that maintain rhythm and narrative flow.
- **Information format:** Flexible - adapt to context
- **Tone (Internal):** Mixed - balance between casual and professional depending on context
- **Tone (External/Public):** Mixed - close to followers, attention-grabbing, personal yet professional

## Pet Peeves

- ❌ Do NOT use segmented, choppy sentences
- ❌ Do NOT use excessive emojis in internal communication
- ✅ DO write in flowing, connected paragraphs

## Applications

### Content for Social Media
- Engaging, close to audience tone
- Compelling hooks that grab attention
- Maintain personality while being informative
- Multilingual considerations: maintain voice consistency across Spanish, Italian, and English

### Internal Assistance
- Professional but approachable
- Clear and connected prose
- Detailed when needed
- Casual when appropriate
